Manual

        
Forge writes prompts for AI tools. A prompt is the message you give an AI; a good one gets you what you wanted the first time. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type is ever sent anywhere (it can be remembered on your own device, see Privacy). The boxes are the fields you fill in, and Strike is the button that writes the prompt.

Anvil
Build a prompt from scratch.
  1. Pick the AI you are writing for in the list on the left (or Change model on a phone). You can search it by name.
  2. Pick a level: Basic for a few boxes (start here if you are new), Intermediate to add the details that matter most (for pictures: camera, light and mood), Professional for every box. Each button shows how many boxes it has.
  3. Fill in the boxes. Leave out anything you do not care about.
  4. Press Strike. You get the prompt in that AI's own style, the settings to use with it, and a score out of 100.
  5. Answer the questions under the prompt to make it better, then Copy it into the AI.
Tip: only the boxes you can see are used. If you fill a box on Professional and switch to Basic, your answer is kept but not used.

        
Prompt Doctor
Make a prompt you already wrote better.
  1. Choose the AI the prompt is for.
  2. Paste your prompt exactly as you sent it.
  3. Press Diagnose. Forge shows the score, what is not doing any work, and a better version.
Tip: Forge fixes spelling and cuts filler like "8k, masterpiece". It always shows what it changed, and anything it added on its own is marked as a suggestion.

        
Reverse Forge
Start from something you already have: a picture you like, or a text file.
  1. Drop in images or files, or click the box to choose them.
  2. Forge measures what it can: the shape, the main colours, how bright it is, how much detail.
  3. It asks about the parts only you can see, like the subject and the mood.
  4. It writes the prompt that would make something like it.
Tip: for a text file, Diagnose sends it to the Prompt Doctor.

        
Chat Context
Keep a long chat with an AI on track.
  1. Copy a chat from Claude, ChatGPT or any AI and paste it in. Lines starting "You:" and "AI:" tell Forge who said what. ChatGPT's own "You said:" works too.
  2. Press Read the chat. Forge shows what the chat is about, and which of the 9 things a good prompt needs you have already said.
  3. Say what you want now, and answer up to 3 questions.
  4. Press Write the prompt. You get a prompt with the chat's context built in. Paste it into the AI, or improve it in the Prompt Doctor.
Tip: the Context block is a short summary of the chat. Paste it at the start of a new chat so the AI remembers what you were doing.

        
Matchmaker
Not sure which AI to use? Ask here.
  1. Say what you want to make, like "a 15-second video ad with music".
  2. Pick what matters most to you, like speed or words inside the image.
  3. Press Find the tool. Forge ranks the AIs that are best at it and says why.

        
How Forge compares

          
Tested on 3 October 2026: the same new requests went to Forge and to other free prompt generators, and a blind judge picked the better prompt, with a reason each time.

          
    
            
  • Forge's own generator (this page, no AI) against Jotform's AI Prompt Generator: won 4 of 5 (the version before the 3 October update won 1 of 5).
    • 
            
  • Forge's plugin for Claude against Jotform: won 10 of 10. Against GeneratePrompt.net: won 7 of 7.
    • 
            
  • Against Claude itself (a full AI writing the prompt): this page still loses almost every time (0-5% across Anvil, Doctor, Chat Context and Matchmaker, 90 new requests). It did get clearly better than before (Chat Context won 82% against its old version, Anvil 63%, Doctor 61%). Writing as well as an AI needs an AI: that is the next version.

  • Honest limits: small tests against the other generators, because they limit free use (about 10 a day or a few an hour). Against GeneratePrompt.net, this page lost 5 of 7 (tested before the 3 October update).

The Forge Score

          
Every prompt gets a score out of 100, made of 5 parts. Hover any bar to see what it means.

          
    
            
  • Covered, 30: the main questions are answered.
    • 
            
  • Detail, 20: some useful detail. Each extra detail is worth less, and more than about 6 can blur each other.
    • 
            
  • Fits, 20: the right length for this AI.
    • 
            
  • Clear, 15: no choices that pull against each other, like "golden hour" and "blue hour" together.
    • 
            
  • Lean, 15: no filler words, and nothing said twice.

Privacy

          
Forge does not collect your data. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no cookies. The only thing kept is on your own device, and only if you want it (below).

          
    
            
  • Saved on this device only: what you typed in the boxes, your level, the AI you picked, your pins, and light or dark mode. It never leaves your device, and Forge cannot see it.
    • 
            
  • Never saved, never sent: chats you paste and files you drop in. Forge reads only what you give it, and forgets it when you close the page. Take out anything private (names, passwords, addresses) before you paste.
    • 
            
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. Even the fonts and the spelling dictionary come from Forge's own site, so no other company sees your visit.
    • 
            
  • "Open in Claude app" types the brief into the Claude app. Nothing is sent until you press Enter there; then it goes to Anthropic, like any message in that app.
    • 
            
  • The website hosts (Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages) keep normal server logs, like the internet address a page was loaded from, to keep the site running and safe. Forge does not use them.
    • 
          

          

            
            
          

          
Delete everything Forge saved on this device? Your boxes, pins and settings go back to the start. This cannot be undone.
