# FLUX.2 (`flux`)

Category: Image. From Forge's prompt library. The 9-part master prompt behind every model is in `../master-prompt.md`.

**What it does.** [pro] / [flex] by Black Forest Labs. Photorealism, skin and material texture, typography, multi-reference consistency, product visualisation and spatial logic. Long, dense, specific prompts are productive here in a way they are not on Midjourney, because the text encoder is a language model. Weak points: no negative prompt on the API, a narrower stylistic range than Midjourney, and [dev] weights are non-commercial.
**Write it as.** Dense prose, 100 to 300 words. Multi-part structure is followed well. Detail is rewarded.
**Settings.** [pro] and [max] expose no steps or guidance; switch to [flex] if you need those dials. Leave `prompt_upsampling` off once the prompt is written. [klein] at 4B runs locally in about 8GB of VRAM.

**Master prompt (FX-00)**

```
A photorealistic product shot of a matte forest-green enamel camping mug sitting on a weathered grey wooden picnic table. The mug has a thin black rim and a small chip on the handle showing the steel underneath. Steam rises from black coffee inside. Behind the table, out of focus, a pine forest in early morning fog. Light comes from the upper left, low and warm, catching the enamel's slight orange-peel texture and leaving a soft shadow to the right. A few pine needles and one dried leaf lie on the table near the base. Shot on a 50mm lens at f/2.8, eye level with the table edge, the mug slightly left of centre with empty table on the right for text. Colour grade: natural, slightly cool shadows, warm highlights, no vignette. prompt_upsampling off, aspect 3:2.
```
