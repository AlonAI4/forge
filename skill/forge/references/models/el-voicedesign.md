# ElevenLabs Voice Design (`el-voicedesign`)

Category: Voice & speech. From Forge's prompt library. The 9-part master prompt behind every model is in `../master-prompt.md`.

**What it does.** eleven_ttv_v3 by ElevenLabs. Invents a voice from a description. Original characters, brand voices, narrators that must not sound like a stock voice. It models the voice, not the space: anything about the room belongs in the mix.
**Write it as.** A description in the documented order: native language and locale, gender and age, quality descriptor, persona in two to five words, two or three emotion adjectives, then timbre and pacing. The quality ladder is Ok, Good, Very good, Excellent, Studio, Broadcast, and naming a rung changes the output. Longer preview text gives more stable results and must agree with the description.
**Watch out.** Never use reverb, echo or delay here. Name the dialect, not "accent".

**Master prompt (VD-00)**

```
Description: British English, London. Male, late twenties. Broadcast quality. A friendly tech explainer. Curious, warm, slightly amused. Medium-low timbre, crisp consonants, brisk but never rushed pacing.
Preview text: Right, so here's the thing nobody tells you about loops. They're not scary. They're lazy. A loop is just a way of saying "do this again, and again, until I tell you to stop." That's it. The scary part was never the loop — it was forgetting to tell it when to stop.
```
