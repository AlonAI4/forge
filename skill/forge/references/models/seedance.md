# Seedance (`seedance`)

Category: Video. From Forge's prompt library. The 9-part master prompt behind every model is in `../master-prompt.md`.

**For every video model.** The one rule that matters on every video model: describe motion over time, not a photograph. Write what happens from the first second to the last. One camera move per shot.

**What it does.** 2.5 by ByteDance. Thirty seconds in one take, the longest of any major model, with identity consistency across many references, product and multi-SKU e-commerce, native editing and extension. Weak point: prompt discipline. Thirty seconds of unspecified time invites drift.
**Write it as.** The whole timeline, 80 to 200 words. Structure: subject, performance across the full duration, ambience, camera, then audio and continuity cues. A prompt that only describes the opening image gives five seconds of intent and twenty-five of hallucination.
**Settings.** 4K and 1080p need mode std; fast caps at 720p. In video_edit mode, duration and aspect_ratio are ignored and you are billed by source length.

**Master prompt (SC-00)**

```
Subject: a young street musician with a battered acoustic guitar, denim jacket, sitting on the steps of a closed shop.
Performance, 0 to 30 seconds: 0 to 5s he tunes one string, looks up at the sky. 5 to 12s he starts a slow strumming pattern, foot tapping. 12 to 20s a passer-by with a dog stops, the dog sits, the musician smiles and plays a little louder. 20 to 26s the passer-by drops a coin into the open case and walks on. 26 to 30s he finishes the phrase, nods to himself, and looks back down at the strings.
Ambience: a quiet European side street at dusk, warm shop-window light, a few people passing in the background.
Camera: one continuous medium shot, slow drift from slightly left to centre over the full 30 seconds, eye level.
Audio: the guitar, faint street noise, the coin landing. Continuity: same jacket, same guitar, same light throughout.
30 seconds, 16:9, mode std, 1080p.
```
