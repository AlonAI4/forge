# Nano Banana Pro (`nanobanana`)

Category: Image. From Forge's prompt library. The 9-part master prompt behind every model is in `../master-prompt.md`.

**What it does.** gemini-3-pro-image by Google. It reasons about the picture before it renders it, so it is the one to use when the image has to be factually right. Legible text in many languages, grounded infographics, native 4K, and it holds likeness for up to five people across references. Weak points: no negative lever at all, a conservative default look, heavy restrictions around real people, SynthID on every output.
**Write it as.** A narrative descriptive paragraph, 50 to 180 words, not a keyword list. Give it something to reason about ("make the ratios in the chart mathematically correct"). Edit conversationally in follow-up turns.
**Settings.** `image_size` written with a capital K: 1K, 2K or 4K. Lowercase 4k is ignored. Do not carry Imagen settings across: negativePrompt, sampleCount and personGeneration do not exist here.

**Master prompt (NB-00)**

```
Create an educational poster that explains the phases of the Moon. Show the Earth in the centre with the Sun's light coming from the right, and eight Moon positions arranged in a ring around the Earth. Under each position, draw the Moon exactly as it would look from Earth at that point, so the lit portion is astronomically correct, and label it: New Moon, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full Moon, Waning Gibbous, Third Quarter, Waning Crescent. Use a clean flat style on a deep navy background with white and pale yellow. Title across the top: "Phases of the Moon". Check that the lit side of every Moon faces the Sun. image_size 2K, aspect 16:9.
```

1. **NB-01 Correct chart.** Draw a pie chart of how a 13-year-old spends a school day: sleep 9 hours, school 7, homework 1, screen time 3, everything else 4. Make the slice angles mathematically correct for 24 hours, label each slice with its name and hours, and title it "One School Day". Flat pastel style, white background. image_size 2K.
2. **NB-02 Cast consistency.** Using the three attached character references, draw all three sitting around a campfire at night in a pine forest, each keeping their exact face, hair and clothes. The tall one is telling a story with his hands up, the other two are laughing. Warm firelight, cool moonlit background. Painterly but clear. image_size 2K.
3. **NB-03 Multilingual label.** A jar of honey on a wooden shelf with a paper label. The label reads "Wild Honey" in English on the first line and "דבש בר" in Hebrew on the second line, right-to-left rendered correctly, in a simple serif. Soft daylight from the left. Photoreal. image_size 2K.
4. **NB-04 Anatomy diagram.** A labelled cross-section of a flower showing petal, sepal, stamen, anther, filament, pistil, stigma, style and ovary, each label with a thin leader line to the right part. Botanical illustration style, accurate proportions, cream background. Title "Parts of a Flower". image_size 2K.
5. **NB-05 Historical scene, accurate.** A busy Roman market street in the first century, with correct clothing, stone paving, awnings over stalls, amphorae and bread, no anachronisms like potatoes or tomatoes. Midday sun, dusty warm light. Detailed illustration. image_size 2K.
6. **NB-06 Timetable graphic.** A weekly training timetable as a 5 by 3 grid: days Monday to Friday across the top, rows Morning, Afternoon, Evening. Fill Monday evening "Football", Wednesday afternoon "Swimming", Friday morning "Run 3km", everything else blank. Clean flat design, blue header row. image_size 1K.
7. **NB-07 Edit turn.** Take the previous image and change the header row from blue to dark green, and move "Swimming" from Wednesday afternoon to Thursday afternoon. Keep everything else identical.
8. **NB-08 Physics illustration.** A ball rolling down a ramp, drawn at four positions along the slope, with velocity arrows that get longer at each position to show acceleration, and a small label "v" on each arrow. Textbook diagram style, white background. image_size 2K.
9. **NB-09 Product with specs.** A sleek electric kettle on a kitchen counter with a small spec card standing next to it reading "1.7 L, 3000 W, auto shut-off". Morning window light. Clean product photo. image_size 2K.
10. **NB-10 Map with real geography.** A simple map of the Mediterranean Sea with correctly shaped coastlines, and the countries Spain, France, Italy, Greece, Turkey, Egypt and Israel labelled in the right places. Flat pastel style, blue sea. image_size 2K.
11. **NB-11 Four-panel tutorial.** A four-panel step-by-step showing how to tie a shoelace bow, numbered 1 to 4, each panel a close-up of hands and laces, with a one-line caption under each. Clean instructional illustration. image_size 2K.
12. **NB-12 Game UI mock.** A game inventory screen: a 6 by 4 grid of slots on the left, a character silhouette on the right, and a stats panel reading "HP 120, MP 45, Gold 320". Dark fantasy UI, gold trim. Make the grid perfectly even. image_size 2K.
13. **NB-13 Periodic table snippet.** The first ten elements of the periodic table as tiles, each showing atomic number, symbol and name, in the correct order and with the correct symbols. Flat colourful tiles on white. image_size 2K.
14. **NB-14 Before and after.** A split image: on the left a messy teenager's bedroom, on the right the same room tidy, same furniture in the same places, same window light. Label the halves "Before" and "After" at the top. Photoreal. image_size 2K.
15. **NB-15 Scale comparison.** The planets of the solar system in a row from Mercury to Neptune, sized in correct proportion to each other (Jupiter about eleven times the width of Earth), on black, each labelled. image_size 4K.
16. **NB-16 Recipe steps, one image.** Three stages of making a pizza side by side: rolled dough, sauced and topped, and baked, on the same wooden board, same overhead angle, consistent lighting. Small captions "1. Roll", "2. Top", "3. Bake". image_size 2K.
17. **NB-17 Character sheet.** A character sheet for a young inventor with goggles and a tool belt: full body front view, side view and back view in a row, same proportions and outfit in all three, plus three facial expressions in a smaller row below. Clean line art with flat colour. image_size 2K.
18. **NB-18 Local storefront.** A small bakery storefront on a narrow European street with the sign "Bread & Butter" above the door and a chalkboard outside reading "Fresh sourdough 8am". Overcast soft light. Photoreal. image_size 2K.
19. **NB-19 Data poster.** A poster showing how much water common things use: a shower 65 litres, a bath 80 litres, a toilet flush 6 litres, a dishwasher 12 litres, drawn as bars with correct relative lengths and labelled. Title "Water at Home". Flat style. image_size 2K.
20. **NB-20 Refine in conversation.** Same image as before, but make the title twice as large, move it to the top left, and change the background from white to very pale blue. Do not touch the bars or labels.
