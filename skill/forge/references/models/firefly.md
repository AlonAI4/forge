# Adobe Firefly (`firefly`)

Category: Image. From Forge's prompt library. The 9-part master prompt behind every model is in `../master-prompt.md`.

**What it does.** Image 5 by Adobe. The commercially safe one: Content Credentials on every output and indemnified training data. Client-facing work, brand-consistent stock-like imagery, Photoshop and Illustrator round trips, in-image text. Weak points: aggressive safety filters that block harmless requests, and it is less abstract than Midjourney.
**Write it as.** Prose in Adobe's own order, 40 to 140 words: image type, subject, action, angle, lighting, background, palette, style. Pick either a prose style description or an Effect preset, never both.
**Settings.** 9:16 is not available on Image 5. For vertical social, fall back to Image 4 or 4 Ultra.
