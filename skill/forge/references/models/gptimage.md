# GPT Image (`gptimage`)

Category: Image. From Forge's prompt library. The 9-part master prompt behind every model is in `../master-prompt.md`.

**What it does.** 2.5 Sunburst / Flare by OpenAI. The instruction follower. Best in class for words inside the picture in almost any script, editorial layouts, infographics, and edits that keep a person's identity. Weak at unforced photorealism (a slightly plasticky, over-lit look), fine-art texture, and specific film-stock looks.
**Write it as.** A labelled brief, 60 to 300 words. It reads sections better than a paragraph. Put literal on-image copy in quotes and state placement and contrast separately. Exclusions go in a Constraints line: there is no negative field.
**Settings.** `model gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`, `size` 1024x1024, 1536x1024, 1024x1536, 1920x1080, 1080x1920, up to 3840x2160 (every custom edge a multiple of 16), `quality` low for layout drafts, high only for the text-dense final, `background` opaque or transparent (png or webp), `n` up to 10.

**Master prompt (GI-00)**

```
Subject: a landing-page hero image for a study-timer app called Focus Loop.
Scene: a tidy desk seen from slightly above. A phone stands in a small holder showing a large circular timer at 24:59. Beside it, a notebook, a pencil and a glass of water. Morning light from a window on the left.
Style: clean, bright product photography with soft shadows. Neutral palette with one accent colour, a warm orange, on the timer ring.
Text: the phone screen shows "24:59" large and centred, and "Focus" small above it, both dark grey on white. No other text anywhere.
Constraints: no brand logos, no hands, no clutter, no visible cables.
Settings: size 1920x1080, quality medium for drafts then high, output_format png.
```
